# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct outlines expectations for participant behaviour and the process for reporting and addressing unacceptable behaviour. It is based on common open-source community standards and is intended to keep the CelestIQ community welcoming, professional, and productive.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, we as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful and considerate in discussion and reviews.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and community.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form.
- Personal insults, derogatory comments, or public shaming.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberate disruption of project communication channels or work.
- Malicious use of project infrastructure, credentials, or data.

## Responsibilities

- Contributors are expected to follow this Code of Conduct in all project spaces and events.
- Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards, enforcing this Code, and handling reports of unacceptable behaviour.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces managed by the project including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, mailing lists, chat channels, and in-person events hosted by the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project maintainers. Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action, which may include:

- Asking the contributor to amend or remove offending content.
- Temporarily or permanently restricting access to project resources.
- Escalation to platform or institutional contacts when necessary.

Maintainers will aim to handle reports promptly, fairly, and with respect for the privacy of those involved.

## Reporting

To report an incident, please open a private issue or contact the repository maintainers directly. If you prefer, you may also use GitHub’s reporting mechanisms for abuse and harassment.

Please include as much relevant information as possible (links, timestamps, screenshots, and participants) to help us evaluate the report.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted for CelestIQ and follows widely used open-source community principles.
